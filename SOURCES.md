Every headline in `entry.png` was checked against mainstream reporting
in the morning editions of Wednesday 25 November 2026. Research was
done by web search in the early hours of 25 November 2026 (CET), so
most stories were reported on Tuesday 24 November in Europe and the
Americas, which is Wednesday 25 November in Asia-Pacific. The two
items dated to Wednesday itself are the Shenzhou-24 launch and the
Asian market slide. Where two outlets disagreed on a detail, the image
uses the version carried by the majority of the day's reporting, and

## The six headline stories

### 1. Trump ready to unveil new tariffs after trade probe
The Trump administration is poised to release the findings of a probe
aimed at countering alleged excess industrial capacity in 16 major US
trading partners, three people with knowledge of the matter told
Reuters on Tuesday, with new import duties on a wide range of goods
expected. The results of the investigation under Section 301 of the
Trade Act of 1974 are expected as soon as Tuesday or Wednesday.

- Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trump-administration-set-unveil-new-tariffs-after-probe-on-excess-factory-2026-11-24/

### 2. Houthi drone hits Riyadh's tallest tower, 11 hurt
Yemen's Houthi fighters struck the tallest skyscraper in the Saudi
capital Riyadh on Tuesday for the second time in less than a week,
wounding at least 11 people. Saudi Arabia's Civil Defence reported
the injuries and posted a picture of the tower, which houses the
headquarters of the Saudi Public Investment Fund. The injured were two
Saudi citizens, two Indian nationals, three Nepalese and four
Egyptians. The Saudi-led coalition said later on Tuesday that the
attack was carried out with a drone and that it would respond
decisively.

- Reuters wire via SRN News: https://srnnews.com/eleven-hurt-in-second-houthi-strike-within-days-on-riyadhs-tallest-tower/
- The Hindu BusinessLine: https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/2-indians-among-11-injured-in-houthi-attack-in-saudi-arabias-capital/article71740913.ece
- TBS News (Reuters): https://www.tbsnews.net/worldbiz/middle-east/11-injured-second-suspected-houthi-strike-riyadhs-tallest-tower-days-1613121

### 3. Pakistan's first astronaut reaches China's space station
China's Shenzhou-24 space flight launched successfully on Wednesday
morning, with its three-man crew including a Pakistani national, who
will be the first foreign astronaut to board the Tiangong space
station. Using a Long March-2F rocket, the spacecraft took off at
8:18 a.m. (0018 GMT) from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Centre.
Zeeshan Ali, a 40-year-old pilot who will become the first Pakistani
in orbit, will work as a payload specialist and is set to stay at the
station for 5 to 10 days, conducting experiments.

- Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/science/chinas-shenzhou-24-space-flight-takes-off-with-pakistani-national-first-foreign-2026-11-25/

### 4. CAS gives Senegal its Africa Cup title back
Senegal were reinstated as 2025 Africa Cup of Nations winners on
Tuesday after the Court of Arbitration for Sport upheld their appeal
against being stripped of the title, ruling that the referee's
decision to resume the match was covered by the "field of play"
doctrine. The CAS panel set aside the Confederation of African
Football's Appeal Board decision that had forfeited Senegal's 1-0
extra-time win and awarded Morocco a 3-0 result. The panel found that
"the referee did not commit any error of law", CAS Secretary General
Matthieu Reeb said.

- Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/sports/soccer/soccer-senegal-reinstated-2025-africa-cup-nations-winners-2026-11-24/
- Reuters (analysis): https://www.reuters.com/sports/soccer/cas-ruling-favour-senegal-reasserts-that-referees-decision-is-final-2026-11-24/

### 5. Shanghai party chief probed in widening graft purge
Shanghai's party chief Chen Jining, a member of China's elite
Politburo, is under investigation, China's state-run Xinhua news
agency said on Tuesday, marking the fourth member of the top
leadership body to be probed since 2025. Chen is being investigated
for "suspected severe violations of discipline and laws", Xinhua said,
a common euphemism for corruption. Shanghai's senior party leadership
pledged loyalty to Beijing on Wednesday after their top official was
put under investigation.

- Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/world/china-investigates-politburo-member-chen-jining-xinhua-says-2026-11-24/
- Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/shanghai-party-pledges-loyalty-beijing-top-official-faces-investigation-2026-11-25/

### 6. Asian stocks slide as tariff threats return
Asian stocks tumbled early on Wednesday as fresh US tariff threats hit
sentiment and a tech-led rally fizzled, with the region's chip-heavy
markets bearing the brunt of the selloff. MSCI's broadest index of
Asia-Pacific shares outside Japan was down 2.3%, led by a drop of as
much as 5.3% in South Korean shares and a 5.1% decline in Taiwan.
Japan's Nikkei 225 fell as much as 2.9%. Nvidia surrendered some of
its gains after Jensen Huang's return as CEO earlier this week,
falling 5.9%.

- Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/world/china/global-markets-global-markets-2026-11-25/

## Method

1. Searched the web for 25 November 2026 world news and collected
   the stories that appeared across multiple independent outlets,
   keeping only stories with mainstream confirmation (Reuters
   throughout; News Agency of Nigeria for one casualty total; Kyiv
   Independent and Al Arabiya for the Uzhhorod meeting).
2. Ranked them by breadth of coverage and consequence, and kept the
   six that led the morning editions across regions and fields
   (trade, Middle East security, space, sport, China, markets), with
   four more in the footer strip.
3. `main.py` draws the image from the stories encoded in it — no
   AI-generated picture, so no garbled text — and the finished image
   was read back and checked headline by headline against the
   reporting above before shipping.
